Privacy policy

Day Strip, an Android todo app. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What the app stores

Your todos: each one's title, due day, an optional due time, and whether it is done. They are kept in a database on your phone. Removing the app removes them.

What the app does with Google Calendar

Only if you connect a Google account, the app asks for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created. With it, the app:

  • creates one calendar of its own, called "Todos", in your Google Calendar;
  • adds an event there for each open todo, changes it when you edit the todo, and deletes it when you mark the todo done or delete it.

The app cannot see your other calendars or their events, and it never reads anything back from Google Calendar. The sign-in goes through Google Play services on your phone; the app does not store your password or any token itself.

What the app does not do

  • It has no server of its own. Your todos go from your phone to your own Google Calendar and nowhere else.
  • It does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Day Strip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect Google in the app's Settings, or remove Day Strip's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Todos" calendar in Google Calendar to remove the events.
  • Uninstall the app to remove the todos from your phone.

Who it is for

Day Strip is a personal app, made for and used by its author only. It is not published on Google Play.
